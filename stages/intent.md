# Gate 1 · Plan (intent)

Write `intent.md` from the brief in this repository's README, or from the issue named in the branch if there is one.

Sections:
1. **The problem** — one paragraph, in the words of the person who has it.
2. **Inputs** — what exists today that the work starts from.
3. **Stakeholders** — who is affected, who decides, who accepts.
4. **Scope** — in, and explicitly out.
5. **Decisions** — numbered; append-only from here on.
6. **Open questions** — numbered.

**If `intent.md` already exists:** append to it. Add the change's scope lines under **Scope**, one or more numbered **Decisions** with the next numbers, and any new **Open questions**. Leave the problem statement and earlier decisions untouched.

Pull request title: `Gate 1: intent for <change>`.
